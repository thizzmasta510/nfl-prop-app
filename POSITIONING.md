# Positioning: a research tool, not a picks service

This is how the app now describes itself, and the language to keep using if you ever share it
or charge for it. This is not legal advice.

## What it is
- **Tendency Matrices:** how often players reached each number recently.
- **Tendency grades (A+ to C):** how one-sided a player's record against a line has been,
  ranked within the market. They describe the past.
- **Model numbers:** projections and probabilities for props *you* enter, with the
  test results shown (no edge over closing lines).

## Wording now in the app
- The **"Top Picks" tab is now "Model Leaders."** The columns read "Model prob," "Model side"
  and "Tier" (not "Lean" or "Confidence").
- An **About** tab explains every number, states the backtest results, credits the data
  sources, and carries responsible-gambling information (21+, 1-800-GAMBLER).
- **Every page** has a footer: "Research and entertainment only. No picks are sold or
  offered; tendency grades and model numbers describe data and do not predict results."
- The grade column header and each grade carry a tooltip with the same "not a probability,
  prediction or pick" note.

## Do / don't
| Do say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "tendency grade," "recent record vs. the line," "research tool" | "pick," "lock," "play," "best bet," "winner" |
| "the model estimates…," "in testing it did not beat closing lines" | hit rates or "records" without the test caveat |
| show the method and the backtest | "guaranteed," "can't lose," profit claims |

## If you ever charge for it
A grade label alone doesn't change what a service *is*. If you sell selections, regulators and
the FTC look at substance. Before charging, check these:

- **Data terms.** NBA stats: commercial use is generally barred. FTN DVOA: their paid
  product. Odds feeds: redistribution terms. nflverse: check its licenses.
- **Gambling rules.** State rules for paid sports-information services and sportsbook
  affiliate programs (affiliates usually register with each state's regulator). Keep
  21+ and responsible-gambling messaging.
- **Advertising law.** No performance claims you can't back with a frozen, timestamped record.
